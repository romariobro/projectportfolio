FAQ

Before we start.

What products are a good fit for the audit?

Existing web, mobile or SaaS products with users, traffic or operating data — especially where growth has slowed or the next priorities are disputed.

What access is needed?

The useful minimum is agreed during scope review. It may include product analytics, acquisition data, roadmap and backlog materials, customer research and focused team interviews.

Is this a UX audit or an analytics audit?

It can include both, but it is broader. The purpose is to connect positioning, journey, UX, funnels, retention, acquisition and delivery constraints around business decisions.

Can the work remain confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality, access boundaries, public case-study permissions and sensitive-data handling are agreed explicitly.

Can you help execute the roadmap?

Yes, as a separate engagement. The audit remains useful on its own and does not require follow-on implementation support.
